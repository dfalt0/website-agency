# Concept 04 — Kiln

## Positioning

**What you are:** A workshop that makes websites by hand and keeps the kiln hot — custom builds, then hosting as continuous heat, not a cold handoff.

**Promise:** Made, fired, finished. Not dragged out of a template drawer.

**Not:** Mass-produced themes. Not cream-and-terracotta lifestyle branding cosplay. Dark workshop energy.

**Ideal client:** Founders and owners who want something that feels *made* — restaurants, makers, clinics with personality, boutique retail.

## Voice & language

| Avoid | Kiln speaks like |
|-------|------------------|
| Fully managed | Kept under heat |
| Template | Stock form |
| Launch | First firing |
| Retainer | Keep the kiln warm |
| Custom features | Hand work |

**Tone:** Warm, tactile, confident craftsperson. Words: *clay, fire, glaze, batch, harden, workshop, first firing, under heat.* Avoid faux-rustic (“homestead,” “artisanally curated”).

**Sample lines:**

- Hero: “Websites made in the kiln — then kept under heat.”
- Sub: “We shape and build custom sites, host them ourselves, and stay in the shop after the first firing.”
- CTA: “Book a workshop visit” / “See recent firings”
- IDs: `BATCH-12`, `FIRING-03`

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Soot | `#1C1410` | Dark base |
| Ash | `#D9D2C5` | Light text on dark / alt panels |
| Copper | `#B87333` | Accent / CTA |
| Ember | `#E85D04` | Hover / highlight (sparingly) |
| Clay body | `#3D2B22` | Secondary panels |
| Bone tool | `#F0EBE3` | Light section bg (cool bone, not warm cream lifestyle) |

**Type:** Syne or Outfit (geometric display) + Source Sans 3. Headings can be slightly tight. Workshop stamps in mono.

**Texture:** Subtle noise on soot panels. Thick borders (1px copper). Cards feel like kiln shelves — stacked, sturdy.

**Motion:** Heat shimmer is *not* a filter gimmick — prefer slow ember opacity on CTAs. Staggered shelf reveals.

## Homepage structure

1. Workshop hero  
2. Shape / Fire / Keep warm  
3. “What we won’t make” (templates, one-week miracles)  
4. Firing schedule (process)  
5. Batches (pricing)  
6. Visit CTA

## Differentiation

Craft authenticity without lifestyle cream clichés. Darker, copper-forward, maker culture — still 100% websites + hosting.
