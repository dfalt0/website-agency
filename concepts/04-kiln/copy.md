## Pillars
### Shape — Build
Strategy and design with your material — brand, offer, audience — then hand-built pages that harden into something solid.

### Fire — Launch & host
We fire on our own hosting. Domains, SSL, deploys, backups. The kiln stays ours; the site stays yours.

### Keep warm — Care
Updates, new pages, seasonal work. A retainer that means the workshop doesn’t go cold.

## What we won’t make
One-week template flips. Sites we can’t host. Projects where you’re shopping only on price.

2. Form (IA + design)  
3. Harden (build)
