# Concept 07 — Practice

## Positioning
Traditional brochure site with a **distinct page architecture** (not a recolor of a shared template).

**Architecture:** Letterhead document on a desk: ruled sections + fee table

**Voice:** Professional practice paperwork.

**Promise:** Website work handled like a practice — scoped, dated, and filed.

**Not:** Experimental scroll toys, identical card grids across concepts, or “same HTML different hex.”
