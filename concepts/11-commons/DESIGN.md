# Concept 11 — Commons

## Positioning

**What you are:** A traditionally structured website studio — familiar navigation, clear sections, restrained visuals.

**Promise:** A common-sense website, nothing fancy.

**Not:** Experimental layouts, bold art-direction spectacles, or “concept site” theatrics.

**Ideal client:** Owners who want a professional site that feels normal and easy to use — not a portfolio piece for designers.

## Voice & language

Commons sense. Teal accent, slate text, friendly restraint.

**Sample lines:**
- Hero: “A common-sense website, nothing fancy.”
- Sub: “If bold experiments aren’t your thing, that’s fine. We build clean sites that answer the basics: who you are, what you offer, and how to hire you.”
- CTA: “Request a call”

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Background | `#F5F7FA` | Page ground |
| Ink | `#1E293B` | Primary text |
| Mute | `#64748B` | Secondary text |
| Line | `#CBD5E1` | Borders |
| Accent | `#0F766E` | Buttons / links |
| Soft | `#E2E8F0` | Soft panels |
| Card | `#FFFFFF` | Surfaces |

**Type:** Heading `"Literata", Georgia, serif` · Body `"Outfit", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Layout architecture

Traditional brochure site:
1. Sticky top nav + primary CTA
2. Centered/left hero with one headline, one sub, two CTAs
3. Three service cards
4. Numbered process steps
5. Three pricing tiles
6. Dark closing band + simple footer

Shared business facts: Build from $4,500 · Hosting $49/mo · Care $199/mo.
