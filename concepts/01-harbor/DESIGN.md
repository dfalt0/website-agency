# Concept 01 — Harbor

## Positioning

**What you are:** A berth for businesses that need a real website and someone who stays aboard after launch.

**Promise:** We design and build your site, then host and tend it — so you’re never adrift between freelancers and hosts.

**Not:** A SaaS dashboard. Not “fully managed cloud.” Not an AI consultancy.

**Ideal client:** Local and regional businesses, professional services, shops that outgrew DIY builders and don’t want another vendor merry-go-round.

## Voice & language

| Avoid (current / generic) | Harbor speaks like |
|---------------------------|--------------------|
| Fully managed | Kept afloat |
| Get started free | Request a berth |
| Enterprise-grade | Steady in weather |
| Stack / infrastructure | The hull and the harbor |
| Launch and grow | Cast off, then we stay on watch |

**Tone:** Calm, capable, slightly maritime without kitsch. Short sentences. No “synergy.” Prefer verbs of steadiness: *berth, tend, weather, watch, moor, cast off.*

**Sample lines:**

- Hero: “A website with a permanent berth.”
- Sub: “We build it from the keel up, host it in our harbor, and keep watch after you cast off.”
- CTA: “Request a berth” / “See how we tend sites”
- Services: “Hull (build)” · “Harbor (host)” · “Watch (care)”

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Night water | `#0B1C24` | Hero / footer |
| Fog | `#E8EEF0` | Light sections |
| Pier wood | `#2C3A3F` | Body text on light |
| Buoy | `#E07A3D` | Primary CTA / accent |
| Horizon | `#5B8FA3` | Secondary links / icons |
| Foam | `#F7FAFB` | Cards |

**Type:** Source Serif 4 (headings) + DM Sans (body). No mono unless for berth IDs (`BRTH-01`).

**Motion:** Slow horizontal “tide” line under the hero; buttons ease like a boat settling. No neon glow, no grid cyber overlays.

**Imagery:** Empty docks at blue hour, rope detail, horizon — never clipart anchors.

## Homepage structure

1. Hero — berth promise + dual CTA  
2. Three posts — Hull / Harbor / Watch  
3. “Who we take aboard” — fit criteria  
4. How a berth works — 4 quiet steps  
5. Pricing as “berth plans” (Care / Standing / Fleet)  
6. Close — “Ready to moor?”

## Differentiation vs current Nodus Forest

Leaves dark-emerald tech aesthetic for cool coastal utility. Language shifts from engineering ops to stewardship.
