# Concept 08 — Storefront

## Positioning

**What you are:** A traditionally structured website studio — familiar navigation, clear sections, restrained visuals.

**Promise:** Your online storefront, kept tidy.

**Not:** Experimental layouts, bold art-direction spectacles, or “concept site” theatrics.

**Ideal client:** Owners who want a professional site that feels normal and easy to use — not a portfolio piece for designers.

## Voice & language

Friendly neighborhood storefront. Warm, clear, welcoming — not cute for its own sake.

**Sample lines:**
- Hero: “Your online storefront, kept tidy.”
- Sub: “A clear website that shows what you do, how to reach you, and why to trust you — built, hosted, and looked after.”
- CTA: “Get a quote”

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Background | `#FFF8F2` | Page ground |
| Ink | `#2A211C` | Primary text |
| Mute | `#7A6A60` | Secondary text |
| Line | `#E8D9CC` | Borders |
| Accent | `#C45C26` | Buttons / links |
| Soft | `#FFF1E6` | Soft panels |
| Card | `#FFFFFF` | Surfaces |

**Type:** Heading `"Fraunces", Georgia, serif` · Body `"Nunito Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Layout architecture

Traditional brochure site:
1. Sticky top nav + primary CTA
2. Centered/left hero with one headline, one sub, two CTAs
3. Three service cards
4. Numbered process steps
5. Three pricing tiles
6. Dark closing band + simple footer

Shared business facts: Build from $4,500 · Hosting $49/mo · Care $199/mo.
