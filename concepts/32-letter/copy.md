## Body
Dear Proprietor,

I am writing because most businesses we meet are paying for a website that no longer does its job. It may have been drawn from a template, or built by a relation, and nobody now has the responsibility of keeping it standing. We should like to relieve you of that. Our practice is simple, and it comes in three parts.

**I. The work.** We design and write the code for your website by hand, for your business alone. You receive a written scope and a fixed price before we begin, and you own the result outright.

**II. The housing.** We host the finished site on our own network. It is fast, backed up each night and watched around the clock. Should anything fail, the alarm sounds at our desk and not yours.

**III. The keeping.** Each month we make up to four changes at your request, apply every security update, and once a quarter we write to you plainly about how the site is performing.
