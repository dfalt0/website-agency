# Concept 05 — Ledger

## Positioning

**What you are:** A quiet digital operations firm — websites and hosting treated like books that must balance: clear, correct, always reconcilable.

**Promise:** Your web presence, accounted for. Custom build when you need it; continuous books (hosting + care) after.

**Not:** Flashy creative circus. Not “disruption.” Not pet-project energy.

**Ideal client:** Professional services, clinics, B2B operators, firms that buy seriousness and hate surprises on invoices.

## Voice & language

| Avoid | Ledger speaks like |
|-------|--------------------|
| Fully managed | On the books |
| Worry-free | No open items |
| Get started free | Open an account |
| Unlimited | Within scope |
| Growth hacks | Steady entries |

**Tone:** Calm fiduciary confidence. Plain English. Prefer: *account, entry, balance, reconcile, open item, statement, retainer, scope.* Never cutesy.

**Sample lines:**

- Hero: “Your website, on the books.”
- Sub: “We design and build custom sites, host them under our care, and keep every change logged — so nothing drifts off the ledger.”
- CTA: “Open an account” / “Review the statement of work”
- Labels: `ACCT-014` · `ENTRY`

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Ledger white | `#F7F8F6` | Page |
| Ink graphite | `#1E2420` | Text |
| Column rule | `#C9CEC8` | Borders |
| Balance green | `#2F6F4E` | Primary (accounting green, not neon emerald) |
| Statement blue | `#3D5A6C` | Secondary |
| Soft sage | `#EEF2EF` | Alternating rows / cards |

**Type:** Libre Baskerville or EB Garamond (sparingly for H1) + IBM Plex Sans. Tables and “statements” as UI motif. Thin rules. Plenty of whitespace — *office quiet*, not sparse luxury fashion.

**Motion:** Minimal. Row highlight on hover. Number counters only if honest. No aurora, no scramble text.

## Homepage structure

1. Statement hero  
2. Three accounts — Design & build / Hosting / Ongoing entries  
3. Sample statement (process as line items)  
4. Plans as account types  
5. Open an account CTA  
6. Footer as colophon / firm details

## Differentiation

Most opposite of Signal and current neon-forest vibe. Trust through restraint. Still clearly websites + hosting.
