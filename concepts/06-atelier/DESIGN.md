# Concept 06 — Atelier

## Positioning

**What you are:** A traditionally structured website studio — familiar navigation, clear sections, restrained visuals.

**Promise:** A calm place to get a proper website.

**Not:** Experimental layouts, bold art-direction spectacles, or “concept site” theatrics.

**Ideal client:** Owners who want a professional site that feels normal and easy to use — not a portfolio piece for designers.

## Voice & language

Quiet craft studio. Warm, plainspoken, never trendy.

**Sample lines:**
- Hero: “A calm place to get a proper website.”
- Sub: “We design and build custom sites, host them for you, and keep them updated — without the noise of trendy agency theatrics.”
- CTA: “Book a consult”

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Background | `#F6F4F1` | Page ground |
| Ink | `#1C1B19` | Primary text |
| Mute | `#6B6560` | Secondary text |
| Line | `#DDD8D1` | Borders |
| Accent | `#3D5A80` | Buttons / links |
| Soft | `#EFEBE6` | Soft panels |
| Card | `#FFFCFA` | Surfaces |

**Type:** Heading `"Newsreader", Georgia, serif` · Body `"Libre Franklin", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Layout architecture

Traditional brochure site:
1. Sticky top nav + primary CTA
2. Centered/left hero with one headline, one sub, two CTAs
3. Three service cards
4. Numbered process steps
5. Three pricing tiles
6. Dark closing band + simple footer

Shared business facts: Build from $4,500 · Hosting $49/mo · Care $199/mo.
