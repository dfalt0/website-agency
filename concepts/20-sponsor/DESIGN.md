# 20 — Sponsor

**Archetype:** Capital manifesto / prospectus. Light institutional.

## Layout architecture
- Every section uses the same asymmetric grid: a 200px **label column** (roman numeral I–VI + section name) and a content column. Nothing is centered; no hero image, no nav.
- I Mission: very large serif statement + two-column position/conviction rule.
- II Vision: statement + a 3-cell bordered horizon strip (Year 1/3/7).
- III Definition: a real criteria **matrix table** (A–F × criterion/test/threshold/evidence) with a dark header row. This is the "criteria grid".
- IV Proof: four-up numeral strip separated by hairlines, plus a bare CSS bar chart (sites held by year).
- V Stewards: full-width ruled **team strips** (initials block, name/role, remit, years). Hover tints the row.
- VI Inquiry: investor-style form (organisation, role, number of sites, term, budget band, statement of purpose, authority checkbox).

## Visual system
Cool white paper, navy ink, one bond-blue accent. Serif for statements, sans for body, mono for labels. Square corners, 0.5–1px rules, no shadows, no gradients. No icons, no emoji.

## YC cue
Housing-scale seriousness (HABU-like) applied as investment language to a web presence: terms, horizons, thresholds, accountability.

## Placeholders
Stats, team names, and thresholds are illustrative.
