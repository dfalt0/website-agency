# Concept 22 — Letter (Letterhead)

## Architecture
A single sheet of stationery on a desk, set as formal correspondence in strict letter order: letterhead (monogram, wide-tracked name, double rule, contact line) → addressee block + date → **Re:** line → salutation → body → Roman-numeral paragraphs → *Schedule of fees* with dot leaders → complimentary close with script signature → **P.S.** (the CTA) → page footer and wax seal. Fold-crease gradients at ⅓ and ⅔ height. Beside the sheet, two **enclosures** clipped to the desk: "What you receive" slip, and a perforated **Reply card** with checkboxes + a "Post this reply" action.

The grid is 800px sheet + 300px sticky enclosure rail — an asymmetric desk, not a centred stack.

## Positioning
Custom websites + hosting + care from a studio that writes to you instead of marketing at you. Cue: slow, courteous, accountable — a counterweight to hype.

## Voice
Formal, warm, first person singular and plural. Full sentences, no bullets in the body. "We shall", "with our compliments", "by return". One dry joke per page maximum.

## Visual system
| Token | Value |
|---|---|
| Desk | `#BCC7D3` slate-blue with faint fibre lines |
| Paper | `#FDFDFB` (white, not cream) |
| Ink | `#14284B` fountain-pen navy |
| Seal | `#9B1C1C` (wax seal, numerals, P.S. underline, reply button) |
| Slip | `#EEF2F7` enclosure paper |

**Type:** Cormorant Garamond (letterhead, numerals, drop cap) · EB Garamond (body, 19px/1.55) · Courier Prime (typed enclosures) · Mrs Saint Delafield (signature only).

## Motion
None. Hover states only (P.S. link tint, send button). Checkboxes are real and toggle.

## Do not
Use cards, icons or UI chrome. No nav bar — the letter is the page; the P.S. and reply card are the calls to action.
