# Concept 02 — Typefoundry

## Positioning

**What you are:** A digital typefoundry — you *compose* websites the way a press composes pages: intentional type, measured layout, durable hosting.

**Promise:** Custom-built sites with the discipline of print and the reliability of a press that never shuts down.

**Not:** A growth hack shop. Not neon “AI agency.” Not a template mill.

**Ideal client:** Professional practices, publishers, product brands, anyone who cares how words and pages feel.

## Voice & language

| Avoid | Typefoundry speaks like |
|-------|-------------------------|
| Fully managed | Kept in proof |
| Get started | Commission a site |
| Features | Specimens |
| Dashboard | Pressroom |
| Optimize | Tighten the leading |

**Tone:** Precise, literary, slightly dry wit. Prefer print vocabulary: *specimen, proof, leading, galley, edition, commission, colophon.*

**Sample lines:**

- Hero: “Websites composed, not assembled.”
- Sub: “We design and build custom sites, host them on our press, and keep every page in proof.”
- CTA: “Commission a site” / “View specimens”
- Section labels: `[SPECIMEN]` `[EDITION]` `[COLOPHON]`

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Ink | `#121212` | Text / dark bands |
| Paper | `#F4F1EA` → **use `#F6F3EB` sparingly**; prefer **`#EDE8DF`** | Light ground (warm but not AI-cream cliché if paired with geometric sans) |
| Rule | `#1A1A1A` | Hairlines |
| Vermilion | `#C23B22` | Single accent (links, CTA fill) |
| Slate note | `#5C5852` | Muted body |

**Type:** Fraunces or Newsreader (display) + IBM Plex Sans. Generous tracking on eyebrows. Hairline horizontal rules between sections — editorial, not newspaper-dense.

**Layout:** Asymmetric columns, oversized pull quotes, specimen cards with service IDs like `ED-04`. Lots of margin. Almost no cards-with-shadows.

**Motion:** Text fades in by line (blur→sharp). Rules draw left-to-right. No particles, no aurora.

## Homepage structure

1. Masthead + commission CTA  
2. Manifesto paragraph (one column, wide measure)  
3. Specimens — Build / Host / Proof  
4. Selected editions (case frames, even if placeholder)  
5. Terms of commission (process)  
6. Colophon / contact

## Differentiation

Trades forest-tech for ink-and-paper authority. Speaks to taste and permanence, not uptime percentages.
