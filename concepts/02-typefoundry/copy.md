Most of the web is set in a hurry. We take the opposite approach: fewer projects, tighter composition, hosting that doesn’t orphan your site the week after launch.

## Specimens

### Press — Host
Your site runs on infrastructure we operate. Backups, SSL, deploys — the press stays warm.

### Proof — Care
Ongoing corrections, new pages, seasonal editions. A living site, not a PDF that happens to be online.
