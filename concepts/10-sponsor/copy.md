# Sponsor — copy

**Masthead:** Sponsor — Custom websites, hosting, and care — on a long horizon. · Open an inquiry

## I. Mission
**H1:** Every serious organisation should own a web presence that outlasts its first launch.
**Position:** We build custom websites, host them on infrastructure we operate, and care for them under a standing agreement. A site is a long-lived asset that needs a steward, not a project that ends at handover.
**Conviction:** Most sites fail slowly: unpatched, unowned, drifting from the business. Sustained attention is the product.

## II. Vision
**H2:** A directory of institutions whose public face is never an afterthought.
Year 1: every client site on a measured care cycle · Year 3: regional cover for firms, clinics, associations · Year 7: a standard others cite when they hire a web steward.

## III. Definition — what counts as a held web presence
A Owned · B Hosted (≥99.9%) · C Recoverable (≤4h) · D Current (≤30d stale) · E Accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA) · F Accountable (named lead, reply ≤1 day). Columns: Criterion / Test / Threshold / Evidence.

## IV. Proof (illustrative)
48 sites held · 99.97% uptime · 11 min median restore · 94% retained after year one. Bar chart: 4, 9, 18, 29, 39, 48 (2021–2026).

## V. Stewards
Principal (Strategy) · Lead Design · Lead Engineering · Lead Care. One-line remit each.
