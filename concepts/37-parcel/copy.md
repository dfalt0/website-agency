## Notice (on the sheet)
**Notice of development.** Land is not built on all at once. Neither is a site. Parcel surveys your business, grades the ground, pours the foundation, frames the pages, connects utilities (hosting), and stays on as the property manager (care).
Click any plot.

## Parcels
**01 · Survey** — Zone R. Area: 1 week. Use: discovery calls, audit of your current site, goals and constraints. Setbacks: we need access to your domain and any analytics. Assessed: included in build.
**02 · Grade** — Zone R. Area: 1–2 weeks. Use: sitemap, wireframes, visual design in your brand. Easements: two revision rounds. Assessed: included in build.
**03 · Foundation** — Zone C. Area: 2–3 weeks. Use: custom code, CMS or flat files as fits, performance budget. Easements: staging site you can click through.
**04 · Frame** — Zone C. Area: 1 week. Use: content migration, accessibility pass, forms, redirects, load testing. Easements: launch checklist signed by both sides.
**05 · Utilities** — Zone I. Area: continuous. Use: managed hosting, TLS, DNS, daily backups (35 days), three-region uptime checks. Assessed: $79 / month.
**06 · Occupancy** — Zone S. Area: continuous. Use: patches, edits, monthly report, a named person to call. Assessed: $249 / month (incl. hosting + 3 edit hrs).
**07 · Reserved lot** — Not yet developed. Use: future sections, a store, a booking flow, a second site. Zoned for expansion; no fee until you build.
