Custom websites · Hosting · Care

We design, write, and run websites for businesses that would rather be read than scrolled past.

One small team. One site at a time. Yours stays ours to look after.

A good website behaves like a good article: it has a point, a structure, and someone who answers for every sentence. We start there. Before any layout, we decide what the page must say and who must believe it.

Then we build it by hand and host it ourselves, so the people who made the page are the people who keep it alive. No hand-offs to a vendor you cannot name. Monthly care keeps the text correct and the machinery current.

The strongest sites we make began as an argument on paper. What is the single claim? What proof sits underneath it? What is the one thing a visitor should do next?

From there the work is craft: type, spacing, structure, and code that loads quickly because it has nothing to hide. You receive the repository and every design file at launch. They are yours.
