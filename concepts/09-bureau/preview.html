Custom websites, hosting, and care — applied for through a clear intake.

This bureau runs a simple public service: we design and build websites for established businesses, host them on infrastructure we operate, and provide an ongoing care desk for updates.

How to apply

Step 1 — Submit intake
Short form covering your business, current site (if any), and timeline.
Step 2 — Receive estimate
Plain-English scope, fee schedule, and expected dates.
Step 3 — Approve & build
Work proceeds against the checklist. Reviews are scheduled, not improvised.
Step 4 — Launch into care
Hosting begins; monthly care desk hours become available.
