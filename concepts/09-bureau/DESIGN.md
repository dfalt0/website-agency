# Concept 09 — Bureau

## Positioning

**What you are:** A traditionally structured website studio — familiar navigation, clear sections, restrained visuals.

**Promise:** A bureau for people who want it done properly.

**Not:** Experimental layouts, bold art-direction spectacles, or “concept site” theatrics.

**Ideal client:** Owners who want a professional site that feels normal and easy to use — not a portfolio piece for designers.

## Voice & language

Civil-service clarity. Blue links, gray paper, no drama.

**Sample lines:**
- Hero: “A bureau for people who want it done properly.”
- Sub: “We take website projects through a simple intake, build, and care workflow — documented, paced, and easy to follow.”
- CTA: “Start an intake”

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Background | `#F3F4F6` | Page ground |
| Ink | `#111827` | Primary text |
| Mute | `#6B7280` | Secondary text |
| Line | `#D1D5DB` | Borders |
| Accent | `#2563EB` | Buttons / links |
| Soft | `#E5E7EB` | Soft panels |
| Card | `#FFFFFF` | Surfaces |

**Type:** Heading `"IBM Plex Serif", Georgia, serif` · Body `"IBM Plex Sans", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Layout architecture

Traditional brochure site:
1. Sticky top nav + primary CTA
2. Centered/left hero with one headline, one sub, two CTAs
3. Three service cards
4. Numbered process steps
5. Three pricing tiles
6. Dark closing band + simple footer

Shared business facts: Build from $4,500 · Hosting $49/mo · Care $199/mo.
