# Concept 15 — Cornerstone

## Positioning

**What you are:** A traditionally structured website studio — familiar navigation, clear sections, restrained visuals.

**Promise:** Build on a cornerstone you won’t redo next year.

**Not:** Experimental layouts, bold art-direction spectacles, or “concept site” theatrics.

**Ideal client:** Owners who want a professional site that feels normal and easy to use — not a portfolio piece for designers.

## Voice & language

Cornerstone. Stone browns, spectral serif, grounded and durable.

**Sample lines:**
- Hero: “Build on a cornerstone you won’t redo next year.”
- Sub: “A dependable custom website, hosted carefully, maintained without drama — for owners who value foundation over fashion.”
- CTA: “Start with a consult”

## Visual system

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Background | `#F5F4F2` | Page ground |
| Ink | `#1F1C18` | Primary text |
| Mute | `#6A645C` | Secondary text |
| Line | `#D8D3CB` | Borders |
| Accent | `#5C4B3A` | Buttons / links |
| Soft | `#EBE8E2` | Soft panels |
| Card | `#FFFEFC` | Surfaces |

**Type:** Heading `"Spectral", Georgia, serif` · Body `"Manrope", system-ui, sans-serif`

## Layout architecture

Traditional brochure site:
1. Sticky top nav + primary CTA
2. Centered/left hero with one headline, one sub, two CTAs
3. Three service cards
4. Numbered process steps
5. Three pricing tiles
6. Dark closing band + simple footer

Shared business facts: Build from $4,500 · Hosting $49/mo · Care $199/mo.
